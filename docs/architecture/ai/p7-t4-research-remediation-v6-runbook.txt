---------------

- Stop if the runtime is not CPython 3.13.15 or the GPU is not Tesla T4.
- Stop if the prompt profile identity or training approval identity differs.
- Stop on non-finite metrics or missing/excess terminal supervised EOS.
- Preserve Qwen chat-template whitespace after EOS in input IDs but mask it
  from labels, so EOS is the final supervised target token.
- Stop if any holdout record is used for optimization or checkpoint selection.
- Do not claim evaluation PASS or promotion from training output.
